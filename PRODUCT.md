# Grant Payad portfolio

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users
People evaluating Grant's website development work and considering a project inquiry.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Grant, showcase selected work, explain his services, and make professional contact straightforward.

## Positioning
Shopify, Webflow, and WordPress development, supported by custom themes, migrations, integrations, technical SEO, and team leadership.

## Conversion & proof
Primary action: email Grant. Secondary actions: view work, read his professional profile, download a public-safe résumé. Proof comes from the supplied résumé and portfolio spreadsheet; individual project contributions require confirmation before detailed claims.

## Brand Personality
Direct, precise, confident. Follow the supplied Parker portfolio reference closely, with Williams' oversized name treatment where appropriate.

## Anti-references
Generic agency card grids, invented performance metrics, fabricated testimonials, and decorative visuals unrelated to Grant's actual work.

## Design Principles
Let the work lead. Keep claims traceable. Make navigation and contact clear. Keep the reference's visual character across viewport sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic HTML, reduced motion, and responsive layouts, as requested in the approved brief.
